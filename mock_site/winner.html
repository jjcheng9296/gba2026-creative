Five questions to ask before your next booking

The company's method can be borrowed by any shipper. A quotation is a starting point. Before you accept one, ask your forwarder:

  1. Who is responsible for each stage? Name the forwarder, the carrier, the haulier and any overseas agent. CE Global books directly with the shipping lines and chooses hauliers for their strength on each route.
  2. Who checks the documents, and against what? Shipping instructions, the draft bill of lading and the weight declaration should be checked against their source documents before filing, and someone should be authorised to correct them. A wrong consignee costs an amendment fee at best and a lost container at worst.
  3. Which charges are included? Ask for the exclusions, validity dates and free-time terms in writing. Demurrage, detention and re-export charges are where surprises hide.
  4. Who handles an exception? A named contact and an escalation route, not a ticket number. One contact. One set of records. One team that stands behind the shipment.
  5. What happens when the plan changes? Alternative sailings, amendments, release requirements and extra charges should be explained and approved before they are incurred.

A useful answer names a person, a process or a written term. Confidence alone is not enough. If you get five good answers, keep your forwarder. If you do not, keep reading.
