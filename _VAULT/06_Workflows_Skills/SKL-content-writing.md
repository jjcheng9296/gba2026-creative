# Content writing (viral-writing study)
Sources: Tim Denning, Matt Giaro, Taktical, Dan Koe — studied 2026-10-18 at James's request. Full notes and the 10 house rules: [[03_Docs/GBA2026_Content_Writing_Playbook.md]].
Core: headline first · scene hook · curiosity loop · phone-sized paragraphs · give the reader a tool · proof without numbers · semi-controversial never personal · faith stated then translated · CTAs as instructions · brand last and complete.
