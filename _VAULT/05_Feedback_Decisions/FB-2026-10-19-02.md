## Lesson
A thin gold `-webkit-text-stroke` on 12 px caps is NOT a border — it fattens every letter and fills the counters, and gold-on-silver has no contrast. For small type make the edge DARK (bevel/extrusion) and keep gold for the big gold words. → [[Design System]].
