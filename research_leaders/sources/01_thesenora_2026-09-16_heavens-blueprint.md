Some lives are built around a career. Others become a convergence of enterprise, service, leadership, faith and purpose far beyond personal success. For SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP, that journey has taken her from accounting, audit and corporate banking into multi-million-dollar entrepreneurship, humanitarian service, authorship, global leadership and what she describes as a divine mandate through Crowned Eagles Global Heaven Throne Room Ministry.

Born in Kuala Lumpur, SHEKINAIH earned a Business Studies degree in Accounting and Finance from Massey University, New Zealand. She began with Deloitte before more than six years in financial control and corporate banking with United Overseas Bank in Singapore.

Beyond the corporate world, she spent nearly a decade serving people recovering from addiction, people with disabilities, elderly residents and families in hardship. During a season when her husband was unemployed, she used personal savings to fund a fatherless young man's university education, help secure housing for his family and purchase a tuition centre for a sustainable livelihood.

"One act of obedience can educate a child, restore a family, build an enterprise and release a legacy."

## Enterprise With Purpose

SHEKINAIH envisioned not isolated businesses, but an ecosystem in which enterprise, leadership, intellectual property, publishing, humanitarian service and long-term development reinforce one another.

She is Founder and Managing Partner of Crowned Eagles Global LLP and CE Global Network LLP, and Managing Director of Crowned Eagles Global Group Holdings Pte. Ltd., CE Global Network International Pte. Ltd., Crowned Eagles Global IP Holdings Pte. Ltd., CEG Malaysia Holdings Sdn. Bhd. and CEG Portfolio Abundant Sdn. Bhd.

CE Global Network grew into a multi-million-dollar international freight-forwarding enterprise built on disciplined execution, reliability and customer trust. For SHEKINAIH, business is a vehicle for creating resources, opportunities, employment and institutions that serve a greater purpose.

## The Sevenfold Heaven Throne Room Mission to Humanity

At the centre of her life is what she describes as a Sevenfold Mission:
- First, advocate between Heaven and humanity.
- Second, bring answers from Heaven to humanity.
- Third, prepare humanity for what lies ahead.
- Fourth, communicate what she believes to be the direction of the God of the Universe concerning leaders, governments, nations and events.
- Fifth, bring healing, miracles and restoration.
- Sixth, bring Heaven's solutions to humanity's seemingly unsolvable problems.
- Seventh, restore humanity to its original God-given destiny.

Her conviction is simple: vision must eventually restore, educate, employ, protect, prepare or help someone recover a destiny thought lost.

## Heaven Decrees Before the Outcomes

SHEKINAIH points to publicly recorded declarations released before major political outcomes were known. According to Crowned Eagles Global's published YouTube record, these included declarations that Donald Trump would serve two terms as President of the United States, Anwar Ibrahim would become Prime Minister of Malaysia, and Tharman Shanmugaratnam would become President of Singapore.

She also cites Cape Town's feared "Day Zero" water crisis as a testimony of divine intervention, noting that Day Zero ultimately did not occur.

For SHEKINAIH, the significance is the chronology: declarations made publicly before the outcomes became visible. Her message is uncompromising, when every human solution appears exhausted, do not conclude that no answer exists.

What appears impossible from Earth may still have a Blueprint in Heaven.
