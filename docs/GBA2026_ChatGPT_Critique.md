### C. 最弱的 10 句（原句 → 改法）
1. "Published features describe CE Global as a multi-million-dollar…" → 用实体 + 年度 + 币种 + 来源的真实数字。
2. "It grew substantially without conventional advertising…" → 营收从 FY[x] 的 S$ 到 FY[y] 的 S$；推荐占比只在有记录时写。
3. "DR J AARON K DAVID brings counsel, steadiness and support…" → 他分担战略、重大承诺与服务标准；在 [某事件] 中他 [具体干预]，结果 [核实的结果]。
4. "The trading company can carry greater responsibility without putting the group's foundations at risk." → "The group separates freight operations from its holdings and intellectual property."
5. "Every hand-off loses information and adds cost." → "Separate arrangements for trucking, warehousing and ocean freight can leave customers managing several points of contact."
6. "CE Global removes the hand-offs." → "CE Global coordinates those hand-offs through one Singapore-based team."
7. "…checked against its source documents before filing, which means fewer amendments, port delays and unexpected charges." → 只保留检查动作；改善效果另写，要有基线和结果。
8. "…a fully digital document trail, so any shipment can be traced…" → "[系统] 在 [期间] 覆盖 [x]% 的货件的 [哪些环节]。"
9. "On digital leadership, it has put AI at the centre…" → "[某工作流] 把 [任务] 从 [基线] 降到 [结果]，覆盖 [n] 票、[期间]，由 [岗位] 批准输出。"
10. "Consolidating cargo … lower emissions per tonne moved." → "CE Global consolidates cargo from several suppliers before container stuffing."

### D. 像 AI 写的 / 空泛 / 重复
- "Converge" 式抽象标题（副标题和结尾标题）→ 换成可辨认的业务成就。
- 美德词反复出现（discipline, trust, reliability, stewardship, purpose, architecture）→ 说一次，然后用事实证明。
- 金句太多（trust / architecture / technology / earning capacity）→ 留最强的一两句。
- 绝对化用词（every stage, every shipment, fully, continuously, never）→ 改成准确范围。
- 结尾的 "On performance / On digital leadership / On entrepreneurship" 自评 → 改成结果 + 具体下一步承诺。
- 信仰与服务可以保留，但要占"证据预算"的小份额；一个由价值观驱动的决策比再多一次宣告更有力。
