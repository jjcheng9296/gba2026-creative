## 2. House style across the features

- **Names:** always FULL CAPITALS — DR SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP, DR J AARON K DAVID. Company names often in caps too (CE GLOBAL NETWORK INTERNATIONAL PTE. LTD.).
- **Her quotes:** printed in FULL CAPITALS when they are display lines (WBI, Insights Success); sentence case in running prose (Aspire, GLV, ACCESS). For GBA, set her quotes in FULL CAPITALS for consistency with the earlier versions and with WBI.
- **Headlines:** three families: "Where A, B and C Converge" (ACCESS, Senora); "From A to B" (Insights Success); "A Before B" ladder (GLV). Also "A [Noun] Who Turned A into B" (Aspire).
- **Openings:** three types: (1) a question ("Every meaningful journey begins with a question"); (2) a contrast that breaks the expected story ("Most business biographies begin with an idea … The story of … begins differently."); (3) a short declarative pair ("Some lives are built around a career. Others become …").
- **Closings:** a measure-of-success line ("legacy will not be measured by … It will be measured by …"; "the question is not simply what can be built. It is what the structures we build ultimately serve") then a ministry tagline. The GBA profile keeps the measure-of-success close and drops the tagline.
- **Section heads:** claims, often two-part with a colon or comma ("Discernment Before Decision, Architecture Before Expansion"), often triads ("The Business Built on Trust, Direction and Responsibility").
- **Rhythm:** short paragraphs; long list-sentence followed by two-to-five-word lines; anaphora ("Understand the numbers. Understand the law."; "I expect accuracy. I expect responsibility."); questions in series.

## 5. What a GBA business profile should borrow

1. **An opening question or a contrast opening**, then a concrete scene. The question form is WBI's; the contrast form is Insights Success's.
