## One desk coordinates the whole journey

Separate arrangements for trucking, warehousing, the ocean booking and destination handling can leave a shipper managing several points of contact, and each one is a place where information can be lost.

CE Global coordinates those hand-offs through one Singapore-based team. The team coordinates sea freight (FCL and LCL), air freight, container haulage and transportation, warehousing and distribution, and packing, unpacking and eCommerce logistics as one service. Customs clearance, handling and cargo insurance can be arranged within the same service, so they do not land on the customer's to-do list.

Its signature is the Singapore export coordinated from pickup to discharge port: supplier pickup → warehouse consolidation → container stuffing → customs clearance → sea carriage to the destination port. Three strengths carry it:

- **Direct carrier relationships.** CE Global books directly with shipping lines and works with local hauliers chosen for their strengths on particular routes.
- **Documentation precision.** Staff check shipping instructions, bill of lading drafts and weight declarations against their source documents before filing, so that discrepancies are corrected early.
- **One point of accountability.** One contact, one set of records, one team that answers for the agreed service.

CE Global does not chase the cheapest quotation in a shipper's inbox. It aims to be the forwarder whose shipments arrive as planned and whose invoices match the quotation.

## A small team, one digital spine

CE Global competes with multinational forwarders that have far larger teams and budgets, in a trade where rates move week to week and a single error on a bill of lading can hold a container at port for days.

The leadership chose to engineer its way through. Repetitive work (drafting, cross-checking, tracking and reconciling) was mapped step by step and moved onto AI-assisted workflows, freeing experienced people for customers, carriers and judgement calls. Each shipment follows the same structured path:

**enquiry → quotation → booking → shipping instruction → document verification → invoicing → payment matching → monitoring.**

AI drafts, checks and tracks. Experienced staff decide. Underneath sit cloud accounting and a digital document trail, so a shipment can be traced from first enquiry to final receipt. In her words, "VISION MUST TRAVEL THROUGH SYSTEMS."

- **Drafting.** Shipping instructions, bookings, quotations and correspondence prepared in standard formats.
- **Verification.** Bill of lading drafts checked line by line against the shipping instruction and commercial documents.
- **Billing control.** Carrier and supplier invoices reconciled against the agreed charges before payment; customer receipts matched to invoices.
- **Monitoring.** Vessel and container schedules monitored, with changes flagged to the customer.
- **Knowledge in the system.** Each step written down as a standard operating procedure, reviewed as the work changes, so quality does not depend on one person's memory.

The two leaders decide what to automate and what must stay human: acceleration without wisdom also accelerates mistakes.
