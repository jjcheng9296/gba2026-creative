## One team, door to door: export logistics from Singapore

An export has many moving parts: the truck, the warehouse, the container, the paperwork and the ship.

CE GLOBAL NETWORK INTERNATIONAL PTE. LTD. coordinates the whole journey through one Singapore team and a local partner network, so you deal with one point of contact:

- **Container trucking:** collection from your factory or warehouse.
- **Warehousing:** storage and consolidation until the cargo is ready.
- **Container stuffing:** loading your goods into the container.
- **Documentation and customs:** shipment paperwork and customs coordinated by the same team, with bills of lading checked against shipping instructions before approval.
- **Ocean freight:** booked with the shipping lines to destination, with air freight when speed matters.

## AI-assisted workflows on live shipments

The goal is simple: catch a wrong consignee on screen, not at the port. The team uses four AI-assisted workflows on live shipments. Software does the first pass; people make the call.

- **Bills of lading** are checked against the shipping instruction (consignee, marks, weights, container numbers) before approval.
- **Carrier invoices** are checked against the quoted rates before payment.
- **Shipping instructions and bookings** are linked to the right shipment record for review.
- **Sailing schedules** from several carriers land in one comparison sheet.

Details matter. According to company records, a September 2026 invoice check flagged a surcharge on three bills of lading that had been billed on terms different from the agreed quotation. Staff held the invoices and queried the carrier before paying.
