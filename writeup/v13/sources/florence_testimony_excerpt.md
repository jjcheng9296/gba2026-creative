# Florence's testimony — verbatim excerpts (business-relevant sections)
Source: https://www.crownedeaglesglobal.org/florence-s-testimony — page title "Florence's Encounter With THE TRINITY IN SHEKINAIH AND JESUS IN AARON K DAVID" (captured 2026-10-20 via the built-in browser, get_page_text). The page calls DR SHEKINAIH SIEW SIN YAP "THE TRINITY IN SHEKINAIH" / "SHEKINAIH".

Intro: "Hello, I’m Florence from Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. I’m a business owner with experience across multiple industries, including education, construction, property management, and professional training."
"I met THE TRINITY IN SHEKINAIH through Making The Stage organised by The Success Resource Seminar in Penang in March 2025. From that day onwards, my life has been truly and undeniably blessed."

1. A NEW WAVE OF TEACHERS FOR MY ORGANISATION
"Before encountering THE TRINITY IN SHEKINAIH, I was at the lowest point in my life and business. My best friend, who was also my trusted business partner, had just resigned along with several other teachers from our tuition centre. I felt completely abandoned, overwhelmed, and unsure of how to move forward. Hiring new teachers was a constant struggle."
"She prayed for me and for my organisation."
"THE TRINITY IN SHEKINAIH spoke words that I will never forget: “The teachers are already on their way.” True enough, within just a few weeks, we began receiving applications from experienced, committed, and passionate teachers. They joined us with the right heart and mindset."

2. FINANCIAL BREAKTHROUGH IN MY HUSBAND’S BUSINESS
"My husband and I run an electrical company. Before encountering THE TRINITY IN SHEKINAIH, it was incredibly hard to receive payments from the property developer. […] Whatever amount we proposed was always delayed, questioned, or significantly reduced, often not even enough to pay staff salaries or cover expenses."
"After knowing THE TRINITY IN SHEKINAIH, she prayed for us and declared that things would change. Miraculously, the very amount we proposed was approved by the authorities without any resistance or questioning. And not only that, the payment was disbursed quickly and smoothly."

8. OUR SHOPLOT LOAN WAS APPROVED IN JUST THREE DAYS
"Before we applied for a loan and even before signing the booking form, I asked for help from THE TRINITY IN SHEKINAIH. She told me the loan had already been approved and agreed upon by THE TRINITY."
"We submitted our personal documents and some information about the property to Public Bank without the booking form. To our shock, the loan was pre-approved in less than three days. Even the banker was speechless. She said usually it takes three to four weeks to get any result or to be asked for supporting documents."

Closing: "My life, my family, and my entire perspective have been transformed. I am forever grateful."
