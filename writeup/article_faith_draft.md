## What a promise looks like in a container

Freight is a promise business. A customer hands you a container and trusts four things: the booking will hold, the paperwork will be right, the cargo will be released to the right party at the right port, and nobody will get a surprise bill.

CE Global coordinates all of it through one Singapore team: sea freight (FCL and LCL), air freight, container haulage, warehousing and distribution, packing, unpacking and eCommerce logistics. Customs clearance, handling and cargo insurance can be arranged in the same service.

Its signature is the Singapore export, coordinated from pickup to discharge port:

**supplier pickup → warehouse consolidation → container stuffing → customs clearance → sea carriage to the destination port.**

Three habits carry it:

- **Book direct.** CE Global books directly with shipping lines and works with hauliers chosen for their strength on each route.
- **Check the paper.** Shipping instructions, bill of lading drafts and weight declarations are checked against their source documents before filing, so mistakes are fixed early.
- **One contact.** One person answers. One set of records. One team that stands behind the shipment.
